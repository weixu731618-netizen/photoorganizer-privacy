Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously.

Photos and Videos

PhotoOrganizer needs access to your photo library to provide photo and video organizing features.

Your photos and videos are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

We do not collect or upload your photo content, video content, file names, EXIF information, album names, or unique identifiers from your photo library.

Usage Data

To understand whether product features work correctly and to keep improving the experience, we collect limited, anonymous product usage data, such as:

  • App version and build number
  • iOS version
  • Device model
  • System language and region
  • An anonymous device identifier (randomly generated by the app; not the IDFA or IDFV)
  • Whether certain features are used
  • Anonymous events in the organizing flow, such as starting to organize, reaching the free limit, or showing the paywall
  • Whether a purchase succeeded, was cancelled, or failed

The anonymous device identifier and device model are used only for product analytics, not for advertising or for tracking across apps or websites. This information does not include your photos, videos, or photo library content, and it is not used to identify you.

Advertising and Tracking

We do not use your data for advertising.

We do not perform cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking.

We do not sell your personal data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store and StoreKit.

We never receive or store your bank card information, your Apple ID password, or full payment details.

Data Security

We minimize the data we collect and only collect what is necessary to improve the product.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how we collect data or introduce new analytics services in the future, we will update this privacy policy and the App Store privacy information as required by Apple.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the developer contact information on the App Store page.
